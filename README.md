## What Is This?

In 1893, Olney Richmond published *The Mystic Test Book*, revealing that a standard 52-card deck encodes a mathematical calendar. The deck is not random. It is a structure:

- 52 cards = 52 weeks
- 4 suits = 4 seasons
- 13 cards per suit = 13 weeks per quarter
- Spot values sum to 365.25 = days in a tropical year

When the deck is rearranged through a specific dealing procedure called **quadration**, it produces 90 unique spreads. Each spread maps to a year of life. The same math governs weeks and days. Every card for every period of your life is deterministic and computable from your birthday.

This library implements the core formulas. Verified against the original sources.

## The Research Question

The card system assigns planetary labels to its grid positions. Each card sits at the intersection of a planetary row and a planetary column. When your Birth Card moves through the cycle, the card it lands on names two planets.

During testing, we observed that the named planets were making tight aspects to natal chart positions during that exact period. Mercury and Mars were named by the card. Mercury and Mars were both within 2 degrees of the natal IC/MC axis that week.

One observation is not science. But it is a testable hypothesis:

**Does the card system's timing predict which planets are astronomically active against the natal chart?**

If yes, Richmond encoded astronomical cycles into a dealing procedure 130 years ago. The 45-card loop would be a compressed model of planetary timing expressed as cards instead of degrees.

This repo provides the tools to test that hypothesis. The math is open. The question is open. The data can be generated by anyone.

## How It Works

### Loops

Every permutation breaks down into loops. The quadration has exactly 6:

**One big loop (45 cards):** After one quadration, each card moves to a new position. Trace where any single card goes across successive quadrations and it eventually returns to where it started, after visiting exactly 44 other positions. All 45 moveable cards share one continuous chain. This is the Magic Circle.

**Two tiny loops (2 cards each):** Ace of Clubs and Two of Hearts swap with each other every other quadration. So do Nine of Hearts and Seven of Diamonds.

**Three fixed points:** King of Spades, Jack of Hearts, and Eight of Clubs never move.

45 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 52.

The total cycle = LCM of all loop lengths = LCM(45, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1) = **90**. This matches Richmond's statement that 90 quadrations return every card to its original position.

The displacement shortcut works because of the big loop. Instead of dealing cards 42 times to find the age 42 spread, you count 42 steps around the loop. Same answer, one operation, O(1).

### Solar Value Formula

```
SV = 55 - (2 x month + day)
```

Maps every birthday to a card. AH=1, 2H=2, ... KS=52. December 31 = Joker.

### Quadration

A deterministic 6-step dealing procedure:

1. Arrange deck in solar order (AH through KS), turn face down
2. Deal 3 at a time to 4 piles (representing the 4 seasons)
3. Stack the piles
4. Deal 1 at a time to 4 piles
5. Stack again
6. Lay into 7x7 grid + 3 Crown cards

One quadration of the natural order (Spiritual Spread) produces the Life Spread. Each subsequent quadration produces the next age spread. After 90, the cycle resets.

## Quick Start

```python
from cardology import CardologyEngine
from datetime import date

engine = CardologyEngine()

# Birthday to Birth Card
card = engine.birth_card(month=5, day=11)
print(card)  # '8D' (Eight of Diamonds)

# Displacement at age 42
disp = engine.displacement('8D', age=42)
print(disp)  # 'TC' (Ten of Clubs)

# Full age spread
print(engine.age_spread(42))

# Weekly card age
weekly = engine.weekly_age(date(1984, 5, 11), date(2026, 9, 9))
print(weekly)  # 48
```
